Submission Guidelines (VLL)

Please review these guidelines carefully before submitting a record:

  • Your video must be uncut and have clicks completely audible the whole way through.
  • Your completion video must be uploaded to YouTube or another recognized video-based platform.
  • **If your run is at or above the level's designated List Percentage (List%), you are eligible for VLL Points.**
  • **If your run is below 100%, and meets the Minimum Required WR, it can be tracked as a World Record run, but will only award VLL points if it also meets the List% threshold.**
  • The LDM/Bugfix copy of a level has to, without a doubt, not make a significant difference in the level.
  • Your record can be valid if it ends midway through the endscreen, ONLY if it's impossible to die in the endscreen.
  • If your record is in the Top 15, you must submit raw footage, with split audio tracks in a downloadable format (e.g., Google Drive/Dropbox).
  • Your record cannot contain any disallowed mods or cheats; Click Between Frames (CBF) is allowed, but physics bypass is not.
  • During your completion, you cannot use skips that make the level significantly easier.

For any questions regarding a submission, please contact the staff via Discord.
