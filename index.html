Submission Guidelines

Please review these guidelines carefully before submitting a record:

  • Your video must be uncut and have clicks audible the whole way through. This ensures authenticity and helps staff verify your run.
  • Your completion video must be uploaded to YouTube or another recognized video-based platform. Direct social media links (TikTok, Instagram) are not permitted.
  • You must have a visible cheat indicator if your mod menu has one. This helps verify that no disallowed mods were used.
  • The LDM/Bugfix copy of the level has to, without a doubt, not make a significant difference in the level. Minor graphical changes are fine, but any change that simplifies gameplay is forbidden.
  • Your video must not end until the player touches the end wall. If it's possible, please ensure that your record ends after the player touches the endwall.
  • If your record is in the Top 15, you must submit raw footage, with split audio tracks, in a downloadable format (e.g., Google Drive/Dropbox). This is a mandatory measure for the hardest levels on the list.
  • Your record cannot contain any disallowed mods or cheats. Click Between Frames (CBF) is allowed, but physics bypass is not.
  • During your completion, you cannot use skips that make the level significantly easier. This includes major route deviations not intended by me.
  • Percentages are calculated in 2.1. Install the 'Percentage Toggle' Mod on Geode to accurately get percentages for VLL levels.

For any questions regarding a submission, please contact the staff via Discord.
